# Business glossary styles (synthetic, for /brief mode)

## Executive brief format (used by Prismatix /brief)
Every manager update is at most 3 sentences and follows this order:
1. The headline fact with numbers (e.g. revenue, tickets, signups).
2. The change versus the previous period or the target.
3. The single biggest risk or next step.
Never include greetings, filler, or "as an AI" phrasing.

## Churn math
churn rate = churned customers / customers at period start (per plan).
Northwind reports churn per plan, not company-wide.

## Example of a good brief
"Q2 revenue reached $48,200, up 12% from Q1. Churn fell from 3.1% to 2.4%.
Two enterprise deals slipping to Q3 is the main risk."
