# Northwind Cloud — Company Handbook (synthetic)

Northwind Cloud is a subscription SaaS product for small freight companies.

## Plans
- *Starter*: $80/seat/month. 1 seat, email support, 2 reports per day.
- *Pro*: $320/seat/month. 5 seats, chat support, unlimited reports, API access.
- *Enterprise*: $990/seat/month. Unlimited seats, dedicated support, SSO, audit log.

## Refund policy
- Monthly plans: full refund if cancelled within 14 days of the charge.
- Annual plans: pro-rated refund for unused full months minus 10% processing.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Escalation ladder
1. Tier-1 support answers tickets in under 24h (target median 1h20m).
2. Tier-2 handles billing errors and API bugs (2 engineers on rotation).
3. Engineering escalation tickets must include: request id, log snippet, reproduction steps.

## Communication style
- Customer emails: short, factual, no marketing language, always confirm the fix.
- Status updates to managers: numbers first, root cause second, risk last.
