# Northwind Cloud Pricing FAQ (synthetic)

Q: What is the price of each plan?
A: Starter $80/seat/month, Pro $320/seat/month, Enterprise $990/seat/month.

Q: Do you offer a discount for annual billing?
A: Yes, two months free (equivalent to a 16.7% discount) when billed annually.

Q: Is there a free trial?
A: 14 days, one workspace, up to 5 customers indexed, no credit card required.

Q: Can we negotiate Enterprise pricing?
A: Enterprise contracts above 50 seats are custom; contact sales with seat count
   and expected order volume.

Q: What does API access cost?
A: API access is included in Pro and Enterprise. Starter has read-only API.
